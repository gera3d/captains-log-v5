First development establishes the proposed use situation and strongest relevant recent evidence; second develops the product experience with 3-4 ordered steps. Preserve all defining mechanisms across these steps. Add at least two consequential proposed decisions, not arbitrary feature lists. Blue and red each have two distinct, developed arguments (paragraphs or items), 90-110 words per team. Synthesis must be last, weigh both cases and name the observation that would change the judgment. No generic seven-day homework plan.
